2. Description of the App

Camille in New York is an interactive bedtime storybook app for children aged 3 and older. The App features:

  • 20 illustrated episodes set in New York City (Episode 1 free, Episodes 2-20 via in-app purchase)
  • 100 hand-painted illustrations in a warm, hand-drawn anime watercolor style
  • Voice narration in 9 languages
  • Voice recording functionality for personalized narration
  • Soft instrumental lullaby background music
  • A poetic moral lesson in each episode
  • Optional iCloud synchronization
